Full-duplex spoken dialogue GPT: multi-stage post-training that fuses speech and text without changing the base architecture.

## Why it matters for Berylize
Shows full-duplex can be post-trained onto an LLM. Context for the Duplug-style turn-taking model in the pipeline.
