## What it is
One-step streaming avatars. Attacks exposure bias (errors that accumulate and become irreversible in AR forcing) with local-future sliding-window denoising.

## Why it matters for Berylize
One denoising step is what makes cheap GPUs viable. Read for the exposure-bias fix, which is the main failure mode of long calls.
