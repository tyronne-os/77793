# UniLS (2512.09327)

**UniLS: End-to-End Audio-Driven Avatars for Unified Listening and Speaking**

## What it is
Unified speak-listen facial expression from dual-track audio; two-stage training learns internal motion priors to solve listener-motion generation.

## Why it matters for Berylize
Listening is the rarest shipped property in open work (see team research). UniLS is the cleanest recipe for one model that speaks and listens.
