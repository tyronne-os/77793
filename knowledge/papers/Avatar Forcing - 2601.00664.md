Real-time interactive head avatar using diffusion forcing, with label-free preference optimization for expressive motion at low latency.

## Why it matters for Berylize
Directly targets the 'feels like a conversation' goal: reactive, expressive head motion with low latency. Preference optimization is a route to tune away uncanny motion without labels.
