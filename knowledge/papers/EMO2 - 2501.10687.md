# EMO2 (2501.10687)

**EMO2: End-Effector Guided Audio-Driven Avatar Video Generation**

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.10687
- Hugging Face: https://huggingface.co/papers/2501.10687
- Published 2025-01, 15 HF upvotes at retrieval (2026-10-02). Group: Expression and realism.

## What it is
Generates expressive facial expression and hand gestures: audio-to-hand-pose first, then a diffusion model synthesizes the video.

## Why it matters for Berylize
Gestures are a big part of 'alive'. The two-stage trick (pose first, render second) is reusable.
