Lightweight 3DGS full-body talking avatar running in real time across devices; distillation for non-rigid deformation.

## Why it matters for Berylize
The cheap alternative: render on the viewer's device, so server GPU cost goes to near zero. Needs a rigged capture, unlike photo-in pipelines.
